How it works

From a brief to a launch-ready brand in three steps.

Your agents work like a small agency. They share context, hand work to each other and check in with you at each decision point.

  1. Write the brief

    Describe the business, the audience and what success looks like. Upload decks, past campaigns or competitor links, and every agent reads them.

  2. Pick your team

    Choose the specialists you need, or create your own. Give each one a role, instructions and boundaries, with no code required.

  3. Review and approve

    Agents post drafts to a shared thread. Comment inline, ask for another round, and export the approved brand book when you're happy.

      FAQ

      Questions people ask first.

      Do I need any technical skills?

      No. You set up each agent by filling in a role, instructions and boundaries in plain language. If you can write a brief, you can build a team.

      Will the agents replace my designer or agency?

      They do the research, strategy and first drafts, which is usually where most of the time goes. Many teams use the output as a sharp brief for their designer, or to get more out of agency hours.

      Can I create my own agents?

      Yes. On Pro and Team you can add custom roles, such as a packaging copywriter or a naming specialist, and decide which files and tools each one can use.

      Who owns the work?

      You do. Everything your agents produce belongs to you and can be exported as a PDF brand book, slides or plain text.

      Is my brand data used to train models?

      No. Your briefs, uploads and outputs stay in your workspace and are not used to train models.

      Can I cancel any time?

      Yes. Plans are month to month, and you keep access to your exports after you cancel.
